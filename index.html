What are Peaked Tiers?
A Peaked Tier locks in your points at a specific tier and position (HT or LT), even if your current tier drops later. The badge always shows your current tier — hover over it to see your peaked tier.
✦ Peaked tiers do affect points. Points use whichever is higher: current or peaked.
How it works
1. Reach a tier in any game mode (e.g. HT1 Sword)
2. Request a peaked test from staff
3. Pass the test → your points are locked at that tier
4. Your badge still shows your current tier
5. Hover the badge to reveal your peaked tier and points
Example
Badge shows: LT1
Hover reveals: ✦ Peak HT1 — 60 points
"sword": { "tier": 1, "pos": 0, "peaked_tier": 1, "peaked_pos": 0 } // pos: 0=HT, 1=LT
Important rules
• pos matters: 0 = HT, 1 = LT
• Points use whichever is higher: current or peaked
• You cannot "lose" a peaked tier once earned
• If your current tier surpasses your peaked tier, current is used
