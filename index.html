How to obtain Achievement Titles
What are Peaked Tiers?
A Peaked Tier locks in your points at a specific tier and position, even if your current tier drops later. Hover over a badge to see your peaked tier.
✦ Peaked tiers do affect points. Points use whichever is higher: current or peaked.
How it works
1. Reach a tier in any game mode
2. Request a peaked test from staff
3. Pass the test to lock your points at that tier
4. Your badge still shows your current tier
5. Hover the badge to reveal your peaked tier
Important rules
• pos: 0=HT, 1=LT, 2=RLT, 3=RHT
• Retired tiers = same points as active
• Points use whichever is higher: current or peaked
• You cannot lose a peaked tier once earned
