What are Peaked Tiers?
A Peaked Tier is a confirmed record of your highest tier in a game mode. To earn it, you must pass a verification test at that tier level.
✦ Peaked tiers are cosmetic — they do not affect your ranking points. Points are always calculated from your current tier.
How it works
1. Reach a tier in any game mode (e.g. HT1 Sword)
2. Request a peaked test from staff
3. Pass the test → your tier is locked as "Peaked HT1"
4. If your current tier later drops to HT3, you still keep the Peaked HT1 badge
5. Points are based on HT3 (current), not HT1 (peaked)
