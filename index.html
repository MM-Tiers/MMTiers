What are Peaked Tiers?
A Peaked Tier locks in your points at a specific tier and position (HT or LT), even if your current tier drops later. You earn it by passing a verification test at that level.
✦ Peaked tiers do affect points. Points use whichever is higher: current or peaked.
How it works
1. Reach a tier in any game mode (e.g. HT1 Sword)
2. Request a peaked test from staff
3. Pass the test → your points are locked at that tier
4. If your tier later drops, you still keep the peaked points
5. Your badge shows the peaked tier with a ✦ star
Examples
Peaked HT1, currently LT1 → earns 60 pts (HT1)
Peaked LT1, currently LT2 → earns 45 pts (LT1)
Peaked HT2, currently HT2 → earns 30 pts (no change)
